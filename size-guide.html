Size Guide

Every product lists its sizes — this guide helps you pick the right one. Measure a piece you already own that fits well, laid flat, and match it to the tables below. All measurements are in inches.

How to measure

  • Chest: around the fullest part of your chest, tape level, arms relaxed.
  • Length: from the top of the shoulder to the bottom hem.
  • Waist: where you normally wear your pants, tape snug but not tight.
