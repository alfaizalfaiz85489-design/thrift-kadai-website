Exchange & help

Exchange policy +

Exchanges are accepted within 3 days of purchase if the piece is defective, the wrong size, or the wrong item — unused and with the price tag still on. Bring the piece to the store or message us on WhatsApp first.

Returns +

Thrift pieces are single, pre-loved finds, so we don't take returns for change of mind. If something arrives damaged, send us a clear unboxing video on WhatsApp and we'll sort it out with an exchange.

How ordering works +

Pick your pieces, add them to the cart and place your order on WhatsApp. We confirm availability and your size, share our UPI ID and QR for direct payment, and hold the piece for you. Delivery dates show on every product — usually 3 to 7 days.

Payments +

Payment is by direct UPI. Once your order is confirmed on WhatsApp, the shop shares its UPI ID and QR code — pay with any UPI app like GPay, PhonePe or Paytm. You can also pay at the store when you pick up your order.
