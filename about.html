About Thrift Kadai

Thrift Kadai is a thrift and vintage clothing shop in Avaniyapuram, Madurai, run by Sabarinathan BRK. We hand-pick shirts, tees, denims and linen pants — single pieces at prices that make sense, most under ₹999.

How thrift works here

Every piece is a single find. We check it and wash it before it reaches the rack, and when a style sells out, it is gone for good. Sold-out pieces stay visible on the website, so you can ask us on WhatsApp to hunt the same style again.

How to order

  • Browse the collection and pick your pieces — sizes are listed on each one.
  • Fill in your delivery details. Shipping is ₹60 within Tamil Nadu and ₹120 outside Tamil Nadu.
  • Confirm your order on WhatsApp. We confirm availability and your size, then share our UPI ID and QR — you pay by direct UPI.
  • Delivery usually takes 3 to 7 days. Track your order with the Track ID from your WhatsApp message.
