Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Thrift Kadai, Madurai · Operated by Sabarinathan BRK

Thrift Kadai ("we", "the shop") runs this website to show our collection and take orders. This policy explains what information we collect and what we do with it. If you have any question about it, message us on WhatsApp at +91 70107 11860 or email thriftkadaimdu@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, delivery address, city, state and pincode — the details you fill in on the delivery page when you place an order.
  • Messages: anything you send us on WhatsApp or email while ordering or asking about a piece.
  • Product photos and catalogue: the shop's own product photos, prices and stock, managed by the shop owner in the owner dashboard.

We do not create accounts for customers, and we do not ask for or store any payment details — no card numbers, no UPI PINs, no passwords. Payment is made directly by UPI only after the shop shares its UPI ID or QR code with you on WhatsApp. Never share your UPI PIN or any password with anyone, including us — the shop will never ask for it.

2. How we use your information

We use your name, phone number and address only to confirm your order, confirm availability and size, and deliver your pieces to you. We do not use your details for anything else, and we do not sell, rent or share them with anyone else. Your order details are shared only with the shop itself (through WhatsApp) and, where needed, with the person delivering your order.

3. Where your information is stored

The product catalogue is stored on Firebase (Google's service), which the shop uses to manage its products. Order messages live in WhatsApp chats between you and the shop. We do not keep a separate customer database.

4. Cookies

This website does not use cookies to track you.

5. Your choices

If you want us to correct or delete the details you gave us for an order, message us on WhatsApp at +91 70107 11860 or email thriftkadaimdu@gmail.com and we will do it.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
