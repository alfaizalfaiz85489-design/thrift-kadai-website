Policies & help

Exchange & return policy +

No exchange, no return, no refund — all sales are final. Please check the size, price and all details carefully before you order.

Returns & refunds +

We do not take returns and there are no refunds. All sales are final — please check the size, price and all details carefully before you order.

How ordering works +

Pick your pieces, add them to the cart and check out — fill your delivery address and shipping is added automatically (₹60 within Tamil Nadu, ₹120 outside Tamil Nadu). Confirm your order on WhatsApp, we confirm availability and your size, share our UPI ID and QR for direct payment, and hold the piece for you. Delivery dates show on every product — usually 3 to 7 days.

Payments +

Payment is by direct UPI. Once your order is confirmed on WhatsApp, the shop shares its UPI ID and QR code — pay with any UPI app like GPay, PhonePe or Paytm. You can also pay at the store when you pick up your order.
