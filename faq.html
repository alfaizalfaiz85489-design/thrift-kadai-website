Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order?

Pick your pieces, fill in your delivery details and tap "Confirm order on WhatsApp". We confirm availability and your size on WhatsApp, then share our UPI ID and QR for payment.

How do I pay?

By direct UPI only — GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or any UPI app. We share our UPI ID and QR on WhatsApp once your order is confirmed. Never share your UPI PIN or any password with anyone — we will never ask for it.

How much is shipping?

₹60 within Tamil Nadu and ₹120 outside Tamil Nadu. Shipping is added to your total automatically at checkout.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 3 to 7 days from confirmation. We deliver across India.

How do I track my order?

Your WhatsApp order message has a Track ID. Enter it on the Track Order page — the status updates by itself when the shop changes it.

How do I know my size?

See our Size Guide. If you are between sizes, size up. Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp with your measurements and we will help you pick.

What if my size is sold out?

Every piece is a single find — when it sells out, it is gone for good. Sold-out pieces stay visible so you can ask us on WhatsApp to hunt the same style again.

Can I exchange or return?

No — all sales are final. No exchange, no return, no refund. Please check the size, price and all details carefully before you order.

Can I visit the store?

Yes — Thrift Kadai, 5th Cross Street, Vallananthapuram, Avaniyapuram, Madurai — 625012. Call or WhatsApp us before you come and we will keep your piece aside.

What if the piece I want is not available?

We will tell you on WhatsApp before any payment is made — so you pay nothing for it.
